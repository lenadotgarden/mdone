Les tests d'écran Mac (`MacScreenshotTests`) passent `-thingsUIEnabled NO` pour rester sur l'ancienne fenêtre. Avec l'ancienne interface, les menus Things sont grisés mais le menu Fichier perd "New Window" (Cmd N est pris par le groupe `newItem` remplacé). | stopgap |

Pas de Trash, pas de listes spéciales (Tomorrow, Deadlines, Repeating, All Projects, Logged Projects), pas de nouvelle fenêtre (Ctrl-Cmd-N), pas de Cmd-clic pour ouvrir en fenêtre, pas de masquage de la barre d'outils, pas de Cmd-clic sur un chevron d'Area pour tout replier. Bouton Partager absent. | TODO |
| 04 section 1 (sidebar) | Counters for Inbox and Today only, hidden at zero. The full tree is shown: loose projects, then each Area with all its descendants (any depth), with a disclosure chevron on every row that has children (always visible, fold state persisted per project id in `AppState.collapsedProjectIDs`, everything open by default, Cmd-click folds all, Expand All and Collapse All in the bottom menu). Archived projects are hidden, their live children surface at the root. Trash absent. Drag a to-do onto a project, Area, Inbox, Today or Someday; drag a project onto the middle of a row to nest it, onto the top or bottom edge of a row to place it between siblings (position written through `PUT /projects/{id}`, loose projects and Areas are never mixed at the top level). | 04 section 3 (to-do ouvert) | `ThingsMacTodoEditor` est écrit à neuf (pas de réutilisation de `TaskDetailSheet`, qui est un formulaire). Il enregistre par `AppState.updateTask`. | 04 section 4 (popovers) | When (Cmd S): header "When" that becomes a text field (natural language through `ThingsNaturalLanguage`, so "tomorrow" or "sept 25" work), Today and This Evening with a checkmark for the current value, a Monday first month grid (`ThingsCalendarGrid`, today marked with a star, chevrons change the month, hover and keyboard highlight), Someday, Add Reminder (time input, stored as an absolute reminder, a to-do without a When gets Today) and a full width Clear button. | 04 section 5 (Quick Entry) | Panneau flottant `NSPanel` (titre, notes, destination Inbox fixe). Ctrl-Space ouvre par le menu quand l'app est active. Pas d'Autofill, pas de tags, When ni Deadline dans le panneau, pas de réglage du raccourci. | stopgap |
| 04 section 6 (Quick Find) | Carte en surimpression ouverte par Cmd-F, la loupe ou Type Travel : listes, to-dos, projets et Areas, tags. Un tag choisi reste dans le champ et montre les to-dos qui le portent ; Suppr sur un champ vide quitte le tag. Pas de "Continue Search" (notes, checklists, Logbook), pas de Cmd-Return en nouvelle fenêtre, pas de headings dans les résultats. | TODO |
| 04 section 11 / 05 (selection) | Single selection. Arrows, Opt arrows (first, last), Return (open), Space, Delete, Backspace and Cmd Backspace are handled by the key handler of the list, not by the menu (a key without Cmd or Ctrl in a menu would fire while typing). Delete removes the selected to-do at once, no confirmation (Things relies on undo); Vikunja cannot undelete, so Undo creates the to-do again with a new id (see the Undo row below). Holding the key does not run through the list (first press only). Space adds an empty to-do right below the selection (same project, When and section, positioned with `moveTask(toPosition:)`), or at the top of the project when only a project is selected; the new to-do is typed in the inline row, not in an open card as in Things. `thingsMacSpaceKey(model:)` extends Space to the rest of the window once the root view applies it (WIRING-AGENT-B2.md). Opt-click on the box cancels. Esc clears the selection. No Shift arrows, Cmd-A or multi-selection. | TODO |
| 05 (move) | Cmd Up and Cmd Down move the selected to-do one place, Opt Cmd Up and Opt Cmd Down send it to the top or the bottom of its section (spec 05). They need the list view of the project (`AppState.moveTask(toPosition:)`) and a manual order, and are ignored with no selection, in a read-only section (Logbook), during a drag, or while the previous move is still saving. System drag and drop (`onDrag`, `DropDelegate`) reorders inside a section with a gap that opens (`ThingsMotion.reorder` spring) and also moves between sections where that makes sense (Today and This Evening, the Someday group of a project), see `08-drag-and-drop.md`. The drop itself is not animated: the optimistic order, the end of the gap and the reappearing source row change in one transaction, so the dragged row lands exactly in the gap with no second reflow or fade, and the server refresh does not animate again (a failed save slides back). No guaranteed auto-scroll, no multi-selection, no deadline or date by drop (Upcoming), no drop in an absent section. Accessibility actions and the context menu offer Move Up, Move Down and Move to <section>. Shift-Cmd-Y ("Move completed to Logbook"): a finished to-do enters the Logbook at once. | stopgap |
| 05 (navigation) | Cmd-Gauche (retour) entre en conflit avec le curseur dans les champs de texte : les boutons précédent et suivant de la barre d'outils existent, sans raccourci. Shift-Cmd-O (popover de navigation), Cmd-L, Cmd-Droite, Ctrl-Opt-Cmd-flèches : absents. | TODO |

Opt-Shift-Cmd-N et Cmd-V multi-lignes : absents. Cmd-D duplique par `createTask` (titre, notes, deadline, priorité, tags, start date), sans la checklist. | TODO |
| 05 (dates, tags) | Cmd-S/T/E/R/O, Ctrl-] et [ (jour, semaine), Shift-Cmd-D, Ctrl-. et , (jour, semaine), Shift-Cmd-T sont faits. Shift-Cmd-R (Repeat) est absent. Cmd-R (Anytime) retire le When (To-do de projet : liste Anytime). Ctrl-Cmd-T (fenêtre Tags), raccourcis de tags, filtre par tag (Ctrl-Esc) : absents. | TODO |
| 04 section 6 (Type Travel) | Fait sur Mac par un `onKeyPress` à la racine, sans combinaison requise. Les premières touches tapées pendant l'ouverture de la carte peuvent être perdues. Quick Find est une carte en surimpression, pas une fenêtre. Pas de Type Travel sur iPhone. | stopgap |
| 04 section 5 et 02 (langage naturel) | Le langage naturel remplace en partie les boutons When, Deadline et tags du Quick Entry. Syntaxe : voir WIRING-AGENT3.md. Les tags et projets inconnus ne sont pas créés. Un rappel utilise l'heure tapée sur le jour du When. Actif par défaut (voir la section Transverse). | stopgap |
| 07 section 5 (sous-tâches) | Le masquage des sous-tâches ne cache plus toute tâche qui a un parent (`ThingsChecklist.isChecklistItem`). Une sous-tâche reste cachée seulement si c'est une étape simple : même projet que son parent, ni start date, ni deadline, ni label, et pas ouverte sous un parent terminé. Sinon c'est un vrai to-do et elle apparaît dans les listes (sinon les sous-tâches réelles de Vikunja disparaissaient de partout). | stopgap |
| Performance (lecture des listes) | `AppState.thingsCache` (`ThingsListsCache`) mémorise listes, compteurs et progression par projet, clé : `tasksRevision`, projet Inbox, jour, calendrier. L'arbre de projets est mémorisé par `ThingsProjectTreeMemo`. Un `refreshAll` en cours est partagé, et le retour au premier plan ne relance un `refreshAll` que 30 s après le dernier succès. | stopgap |
| 04 section 2 (Today, événements) | La boîte d'événements du calendrier est faite. Elle n'apparaît que si l'accès EventKit est déjà accordé : la liste ne demande jamais la permission. | stopgap |

## Undo, completion and drop animation (Mac list)

Code written without a compiler, to be validated by the macOS CI and on a Mac.

| Spec | Deviation | Status |
|-|-|-|
| 05 (Cmd Z, Shift Cmd Z) | Edit > Undo and Redo name the step ("Undo Complete To-Do"). History: `ThingsUndoStack` (pure, `Features/Things`, tested), depth 100, rapid edits with the same key within 1.5 s merge into one step (Ctrl ] pressed five times, a burst of reorders), cleared on logout (`ThingsMacModel.clearUndoHistory`, and every undo and record checks `isAuthenticated`). Covered: complete and reopen, cancel, delete, create, duplicate, move to a project or Area, move of a project, reorder and move between sections, When, Deadline, reminder, title and notes of the card, tags, checklist items (add, tick, rename, delete). Vikunja cannot undelete: Undo Delete creates the to-do again (title, notes, deadline, start date, tags including the This Evening and Someday labels, priority, project, repeat, checklist, done state) and the id changes; older steps follow it through an alias table. The recreated to-do goes where the server puts a new task, not back at its old position; comments, attachments, reminders, assignees and the creation date are lost. A focused text field keeps its own undo (the menu action forwards `undo:` to it when a text view is first responder; the items stay enabled while a card, the new to-do row, Quick Find or a sheet is open). Without a Things window (classic interface) the items forward to the responder chain. Not covered: Opt-click cancel through other paths, sidebar renames, New Project and Area creation. | stopgap |
| 06 section 2 (completion, Mac) | Ticking the box (click, Cmd K, the card checkbox) fills the box and draws the checkmark in 0.25 s (`ThingsMotion.checkDraw`, a `trim` of a path), the title greys and a strike line grows over it (0.2 s, an overlay instead of `strikethrough`, which cannot animate), the row stays 1 s (`completionRemovalDelay`) and then collapses (`rowRemoval`). A second tick during that second cancels. With Reduce Motion every step is immediate. The iPhone row keeps the text strikethrough. | stopgap |
